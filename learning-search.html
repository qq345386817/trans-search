Find learning resources in another language

Find a different perspective on a topic without installing an extension. Start with a short query and review the translation before opening results.

A three-step workflow

  1. Name the topic and your level: beginner, introduction, or advanced. Avoid putting personal details into the query.
  2. Choose a language you want to explore. Review the translated topic and keep mathematical notation and product names intact.
  3. Compare multiple resources. Check who published them, their prerequisites, and whether their examples match your needs.
