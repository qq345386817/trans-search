Privacy

Local translation keeps the query on your device. Choosing Google online sends it to Google. Clicking a search link sends it to that engine. No account or saved query history. The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for visits and performance.

This translates the query, not the results page. Translation service availability can vary.

Why this exists

Useful information is not distributed evenly across the web. Some topics are better covered in different languages, and searching in only one language can leave good results behind.

Transparent by design

Your queries stay under your control.

Translate into English, Chinese, Japanese and more, then choose Google, Bing, Baidu, Sogou, DuckDuckGo, Naver, Brave Search or Yandex to continue your research. Bring the same flow into your browser. The Safari companion app helps you enable the extension; the Chrome extension translates queries automatically or on demand on supported search pages.

On-device web default

Defaults to browser built-in Translator and LanguageDetector APIs so your query stays local where supported.

Explicit online choice

Online translation on the web or in the extension runs only when you choose or authorize it—and can be revoked anytime.

No account or saved history

No login, no query database, and no custom tracking. Website metrics use only official Cloudflare Web Analytics.
