This translates the query, not the results page. Translation service availability can vary.

Why this exists

Useful information is not distributed evenly across the web. Some topics are better covered in different languages, and searching in only one language can leave good results behind.

Supported search pages

The website can open Google, Bing, Baidu and Sogou searches without an extension. The companion app and Safari/Chromium extension are in development and provide translation directly in supported browser search pages.
